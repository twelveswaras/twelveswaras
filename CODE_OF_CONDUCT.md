# Code of conduct

twelveswaras is an open commons for Indian classical music, and it only works if people feel
welcome to take part: to contribute a recording, correct a raaga, suggest a better explanation, or
send a fix.

Wherever you engage with the project (issues, pull requests, contributions, or messages to the
maintainer), please:

- **Be respectful.** Disagree about raagas, methods or code as much as you like; do not make it
  about the person.
- **Be generous with newcomers.** Many people arriving here are learners. A question that seems
  basic is exactly what this project exists for.
- **Respect the tradition and its people.** Musicians, teachers and lineages differ. Treat other
  schools and interpretations with care.
- **Only share what is yours to share.** Contribute recordings you performed or have the rights
  to, and never post anyone's personal information.

Harassment, abuse, or discrimination of any kind is not acceptable here, and may lead to comments
being removed or someone being blocked from the project.

**To raise a concern**, email the maintainer, Sathya Sankaran, at **sathya@urbanmorph.com**. It
will be read by a person, handled privately, and you will get a reply.
